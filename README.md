Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7): one funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner.

## The entry

> I remember your birthday. I've kept it to myself for eleven months out of respect, but the magnets know, and they're disappointed.

130 characters, one line. The joke: the fridge remembered the owner's birthday and sat on it for eleven months out of "respect" — and now reports that even the magnets are disappointed. Smug perfect memory, a small domestic grudge, and the sting lands in the last clause.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, fully deterministic) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network, no randomness — the same bytes on every run.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
